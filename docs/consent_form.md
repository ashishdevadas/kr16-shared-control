# Participant information and consent form

**Study:** Assisted teleoperation of a robot arm for inspection and repair in hazardous facilities (simulation)
**Researcher:** Ashish Devadas, MSc Control & Robotics (CORO-IMARO), École Centrale de Nantes
**Supervisor:** ______________________

## What is this study about?

We compare three ways a computer can help a person remotely drive a robot arm towards a defect on a piece of equipment: no help, safety limits, and automatic guidance. The robot exists only in a computer simulation. No real robot moves.

## What will I do?

- You will drive the simulated robot with the keyboard while watching it on the screen. You will do 3 short blocks of about 6 trials each.
- After each block you will answer 6 short questions about how demanding the task felt. At the end you will say which version you preferred.
- The session takes about **45 minutes**.

## Risks and benefits

- There is no physical risk. You may find the task tiring or frustrating at times, and you can take a break whenever you want.
- There is no direct benefit to you. The results help to design safer remote-handling robots.

## Your data

- The computer records the robot's motion, your task times and errors, and your questionnaire answers.
- Your data are stored under a code (for example P04), never under your name. No video, audio or photo is recorded.
- The anonymous results may be used in a student report, a public code repository and a scientific publication.
- This signed form is kept separately from the data.
